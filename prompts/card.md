# Holiday card prompts (Nano Banana in Photoshop)

Same rules as the sweater: one change per generation, say what to keep, fix small areas with Generative Fill.

---

## C1. The knitted living room (Generate Image, landscape 7:5 or 3:2)
```
A cozy living room on Christmas Eve where absolutely everything is hand-knitted from chunky wool: the Christmas tree and its ornaments, the wrapped presents, the fireplace and its knitted orange flames, the armchair, a rug, a ginger cat curled on the rug, and big knitted snowflakes falling outside a knitted window. Colours: cranberry red, pine green, cream, mustard yellow and charcoal, with warm lamp light. Whimsical stop-motion look, shallow depth of field. On the mantel, an empty gold picture frame. Leave clear space in the centre foreground for a person standing.
```
**Check:** everything really is knitted (look at the tree, the flames and the window). If something looks plastic, select it and Generative Fill: `same object but hand-knitted from chunky wool with visible V-shaped stitches`.

## C2. Put yourself in (your photo, then Generative Fill with the sweater as reference)
1. Place your photo, **Select Subject**, add a layer mask. Scale so you stand in the centre foreground.
2. Select your torso and Generative Fill with **the finished sweater PNG as the reference image**:
```
Dress this person in the exact ugly Christmas sweater from the reference image, including the knitted portrait in the centre, fitted naturally with folds. Keep the face, hair and pose exactly the same.
```
3. Then select a soft area around your feet and edges:
```
Blend this person into the room: matching warm lamp light from the left, a soft contact shadow on the knitted rug, and a warm glow from the fireplace on one side.
```

## C3. The cat has the yarn (Generative Fill around the cat)
```
The same ginger cat, now sitting up and holding a long strand of cranberry-red yarn in its mouth. The yarn leads across the rug towards the person's sweater hem.
```

## C4. The card inside the card (Photoshop, no generation)
See PLAYBOOK step 8: the finished card goes into the mantel frame as a Smart Object.

## C5. The banner (Generative Fill, a wide strip above the fireplace or across the top)
```
A knitted bunting banner hanging across the room, cream letters knitted on red triangular flags, spelling: WISHING YOU AN INFINITELY COZY HOLIDAY
```
If the text isn't perfect after two tries: type it in Photoshop with a chunky rounded font, warp it (Edit > Transform > Warp > Arc, small bend), then run C6 on just the text.

## C6. Knit-ify typed text (Generative Fill over your text layer, after merging a copy)
```
Make these letters look hand-knitted in cream wool on red knitted flags, with V-shaped stitches, keeping the exact same letters and spelling.
```

## C7. Corner signature (type layer, no generation)
Small, bottom right, in the cream colour: `From all of us. (It's all me.)`

### Spare prompts
- **Snow glow:** `Make the knitted snowflakes outside the window glow faintly in the moonlight. Keep everything else the same.`
- **Stockings:** `Add three knitted stockings on the mantel, each with a tiny knitted portrait of the same person.`
- **Ornament joke:** `Turn one ornament on the tree into a tiny knitted version of the same ugly sweater.`
