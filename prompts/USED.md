# The exact Nano Banana Pro prompts used

All edits used **Gemini 3 (with Nano Banana Pro)**, selected as the partner model in Photoshop (web) Generative Fill.

## Sweater

**1. Whole canvas** (Select All → Generative Fill)
```
Turn this into the ugliest, most gloriously over-decorated Christmas sweater ever, as a flat-lay product photo on the same light grey background. Keep the same red and pine-green chunky knit, the snowflakes and the two big cream reindeer, and keep the person's face recognisable. Replace the small photo in the centre with a much larger square knitted intarsia portrait of the same person, head and shoulders, smiling, wearing this exact same ugly sweater, knitted in the same chunky yarn with visible V-shaped stitches. Add: make the reindeer cross-eyed and flip one so it faces the wrong way; a glossy red 3D felt pom-pom nose sewn onto one reindeer; a knitted string of Christmas lights across the chest with tiny glowing red, green and yellow LED bulbs sewn on; fluffy white pom-pom snowballs stuck on at random; gold tinsel fringe along the cuffs and the hem; the words I AM THE SWEATER knitted in cream letters on the red hem band; and a hole near the bottom left where the knit has unravelled, with a fluffy ginger cat paw poking through pulling a long loose strand of red yarn.
```

**2. Marquee on just the portrait panel** (the recursion)
```
Inside this knitted portrait, show the same person from the chest up wearing a smaller version of this exact ugly Christmas sweater, red and green with the Christmas lights, pom-poms and reindeer, and on its chest a tiny knitted portrait of herself wearing the sweater again. Keep her face, hair and smile exactly the same. Everything inside the panel is knitted in the same chunky yarn with visible V-shaped stitches.
```

Photoshop: each generation on its own masked, named layer; Vibrance adjustment layer +25.

## Holiday card

Save As a copy of the sweater file, so the finished sweater is on the canvas as the reference.

**1. Whole canvas**
```
Turn this into a finished square holiday greeting card photo. The woman whose face is knitted into the sweater portrait now stands in the centre as a real person, smiling, wearing this exact ugly Christmas sweater with every detail kept: the knitted portrait panel of herself, the Christmas lights, pom-poms, gold tinsel, cross-eyed reindeer with the red nose, the ginger cat paw and I AM THE SWEATER on the hem. Keep her face exactly like the portrait. She is in a cozy living room on Christmas Eve where absolutely everything is hand-knitted from chunky wool: the Christmas tree and ornaments, wrapped presents, a fireplace with knitted orange flames, an armchair, a rug, and big knitted snowflakes falling outside a knitted window. A fluffy ginger cat sits at her feet holding a strand of red yarn that leads to her sweater hem. On the mantel, a small gold frame shows a tiny copy of this same card. Across the top hangs knitted bunting with cream letters on red flags spelling WISHING YOU AN INFINITELY COZY HOLIDAY. Warm lamp light, cranberry red, pine green, cream and mustard colours, whimsical stop-motion look.
```

**2. Marquee on just the head** (face consistency)
```
Make this woman's face and hair match the knitted portrait on her own sweater exactly: the same adult face, eyes, eyebrows, nose and smile, and long straight dark hair falling past her shoulders, no braids. Keep the same pose, lighting and warm room colours.
```

Photoshop: the type layer "From all of us. It's all me." in cream, placed with the Move tool; the shared Vibrance adjustment; named layers.

Four Nano Banana Pro generations in total.
