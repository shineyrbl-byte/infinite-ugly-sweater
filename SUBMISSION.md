## How I used Nano Banana and Photoshop: the sweater

```
I wanted an ugly sweater that's ugly in every direction at once, and that contains itself.

I started with a red-and-green reindeer sweater, an empty cream panel on the chest and a reference photo of myself in that panel. Then I switched Generative Fill to Gemini 3 with Nano Banana Pro and did the rest with it:

1. One whole-sweater edit to make it as ugly as possible while keeping the knit, the reindeer and my likeness: a string of glowing LED Christmas lights, white pom-poms stuck on at random, gold tinsel fringe on the cuffs and hem, cross-eyed reindeer, one with a glossy 3D felt nose, a ginger cat's paw unravelling the sweater through a hole near the hem, and "I AM THE SWEATER" knitted into the hem band. The same edit turned my photo into a large knitted intarsia portrait with real V-shaped stitches. Nano Banana Pro kept my face recognisable in yarn.
2. The recursion: I selected just the portrait panel and asked Nano Banana Pro to show me wearing a smaller copy of this exact sweater, with a tiny portrait of myself on its chest. So the sweater shows me wearing the sweater, which shows me wearing the sweater.

In Photoshop, each generation lives on its own layer with its mask, so the base, the portrait and the recursion stay separate and editable. A Vibrance adjustment layer on top brings the reds, greens and the LED glow together.
```

## How I used Nano Banana and Photoshop: the holiday card

```
The card continues the joke: I'm standing in a living room where everything is knitted, wearing the sweater, and the picture on the mantel is the card itself.

1. I used Save As to make a copy of my sweater file, so the finished sweater was on the canvas. Then I ran Gemini 3 with Nano Banana Pro over the whole canvas, using the sweater as its reference. It put me, as a real person, in the centre wearing that exact sweater, with every detail kept (the knitted portrait, the lights, the tinsel, "I AM THE SWEATER"). Around me it built a living room where everything is hand-knitted: the tree and ornaments, the presents, the fireplace with knitted flames, the armchair, the rug and the snowflakes outside the window. The ginger cat from the sweater's hole is now on the rug, holding the red yarn that leads back to my hem. A tiny copy of the card sits in a gold frame on the mantel, and knitted bunting reads "WISHING YOU AN INFINITELY COZY HOLIDAY", spelled correctly by Nano Banana Pro.
2. My face drifted a little in the big edit, so I selected just my head and used Nano Banana Pro again to match my face and hair to the knitted portrait on my own sweater.

In Photoshop I added the signature "From all of us. It's all me." as a real type layer in a cream colour picked from the palette, and kept the Vibrance adjustment from the sweater file so both images share the same colour treatment. Every AI edit is a separate named layer, as the Layers screenshot shows.
```
